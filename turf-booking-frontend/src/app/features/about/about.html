About TurfXpert

TurfXpert is your ultimate platform for discovering, booking, and managing premium sports arenas.

Founded with a passion for bringing athletes together, TurfXpert bridges the gap between sports enthusiasts and facility owners. We believe that finding a great place to play shouldn't be a hassle. Whether you are looking for a quick 5-a-side football game, a pristine cricket pitch, or a top-tier tennis court, we've got you covered.

For Players

Explore verified turfs in your area, view real-time availability, and book instantly. No more calling around or dealing with double bookings.

For Owners

Manage your facility effortlessly with our dedicated Owner Dashboard. Track bookings, manage revenue, and grow your customer base with our automated tools.

Our mission is to foster a vibrant, active community by making sports facilities accessible to everyone. We continually innovate our platform to provide you with the best user experience, from secure payments to split-billing and instant notifications.

Get out there and play. We'll handle the rest.
