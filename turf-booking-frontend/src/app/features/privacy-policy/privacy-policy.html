Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, book a turf, or interact with our platform, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and payment details. We also collect usage data to improve our services and provide a personalized experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Process your turf bookings and transactions.
  • Send you booking confirmations and important updates.
  • Facilitate communication between players and turf owners.
  • Improve the functionality and security of the TurfXpert platform.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share necessary information (such as your name and contact number) with the specific Turf Owners you book with to ensure a smooth check-in process. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our platform and hold certain information. This helps us analyze web traffic and improve our user interface.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can manage your preferences from your Profile dashboard or contact our support team for assistance.
